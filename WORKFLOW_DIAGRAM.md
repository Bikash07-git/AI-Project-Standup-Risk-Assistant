## Explanation

This workflow keeps the product focused on the real operational problem: reducing manual follow-up and speeding up risk recognition. Employees submit short updates using a standardized structure or free-form text that is converted into a consistent format. The AI then extracts tasks, blockers, dependencies, and risk, followed by confidence assessment and human validation when needed. The result feeds manager dashboards and executive metrics without requiring a broad project-management platform.
