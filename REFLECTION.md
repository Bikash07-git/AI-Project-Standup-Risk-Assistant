# Stage 1 Reflection

## Why This Solution Was Chosen

The assignment focuses on one central pain point: managers spend too much time collecting updates and identifying blockers. The solution keeps scope narrow and useful by emphasizing structured updates, AI extraction, recurring blocker detection, and manager-facing summaries.

## Assumptions Made

- Employees are more likely to provide useful updates when the form is short and guided.
- Managers want a compact operational overview rather than a large PM tool.
- AI should assist decision-making but not replace managerial judgment.
- A deterministic demo mode is acceptable for the assignment because the priority is demonstrating the workflow clearly and reliably.

## Trade-Offs Made

- The product is intentionally not a full PM platform.
- AI analysis is lightweight and deterministic rather than deeply model-driven.
- Dashboard logic favors clarity and demonstration quality over enterprise-scale complexity.

## What Would Improve with Another Week

- Real provider-based LLM integration and prompt evaluation
- Persistent SQLite storage for all updates and reviews
- More advanced trend analysis for recurring blockers and risk cycles
- More polished reporting and export flows for leadership packs
