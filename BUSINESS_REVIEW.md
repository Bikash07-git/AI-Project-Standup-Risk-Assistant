# 30-Day Business Review

## A. Top 5 Challenges

### 1. Fragmented project status updates

- Challenge: Updates exist across chat, email, and meetings.
- Evidence: The scenario describes scattered project updates across channels.
- Likely root cause: No single intake mechanism or standard format.
- Impact: Managers spend time chasing updates instead of acting on risk.

### 2. Inconsistent reporting quality

- Challenge: Teams use different styles and details in updates.
- Evidence: The assignment highlights inconsistent reporting styles and free-form updates.
- Likely root cause: No standardized input workflow.
- Impact: AI and managers cannot reliably compare status across teams.

### 3. Slow blocker identification

- Challenge: Managers have to infer which issues are blockers and what is affected.
- Evidence: The assignment explicitly calls out blocker detection as the main value driver.
- Likely root cause: Blockers are buried inside natural-language updates.
- Impact: Risks are discovered late and escalate slower than needed.

### 4. Limited trust in AI output

- Challenge: AI sometimes misses context or misclassifies blockers.
- Evidence: The scenario notes AI may miss context and incorrectly identify blockers.
- Likely root cause: No human review, confidence threshold, or validation loop.
- Impact: Wrong recommendations can reduce trust and adoption.

### 5. Weak measurement of adoption and quality

- Challenge: Leadership cannot easily track operational reliability.
- Evidence: The assignment calls for adoption, correction rate, confirmation rate, and leadership metrics.
- Likely root cause: Metrics are not captured in a consistent system.
- Impact: Innovation is hard to justify without measurable outcomes.

## B. Top 3 Priorities

### 1. Standardize updates and intake

- Reason: Consistent reports are a prerequisite for reliable AI analysis.
- Expected impact: Better quality data and higher trust.
- Why first: Without structure, the whole workflow remains noisy and inconsistent.

### 2. Add human validation and confidence checks

- Reason: AI should support decisions, not blindly act on uncertain signals.
- Expected impact: Lower false positives and better manager confidence.
- Why second: It directly addresses trust issues that threaten adoption.

### 3. Measure adoption and operational quality

- Reason: Leadership needs reliable metrics, not anecdotal impressions.
- Expected impact: Better governance and faster optimization cycles.
- Why third: Measurement makes the process sustainable beyond a pilot.

## C. Improved Solution

The redesign addresses the three priorities by combining structured update intake with free-form normalization, recurring-context risk detection, and manager validation steps. The system keeps the workflow practical by helping employees submit updates in a consistent format, letting AI infer tasks and blockers with explicit confidence, and enabling managers to confirm, edit, reject, or resolve conclusions inside the same workflow. Leadership metrics then provide a simple view of adoption, completion, AI quality feedback, and operational impact.

## D. Five Success Metrics

### 1. Adoption Rate

- Definition: Percentage of expected updates submitted by active employees.
- Calculation: Submitted updates / expected updates.
- Why leadership should track it: Measures whether the process is actually used.
- Target direction: Upward.

### 2. Update Completion Rate

- Definition: Percentage of reports with complete required fields.
- Calculation: Complete submissions / total submissions.
- Why leadership should track it: Measures reporting quality and data reliability.
- Target direction: Upward.

### 3. Blocker Confirmation Rate

- Definition: Percentage of AI-detected blockers confirmed or edited by managers.
- Calculation: Confirmed or approved blockers / total detected blockers.
- Why leadership should track it: Measures AI usefulness and trust.
- Target direction: Upward.

### 4. AI Correction Rate

- Definition: Percentage of AI outputs changed by managers.
- Calculation: Managed corrections / total reviewed AI outputs.
- Why leadership should track it: Shows whether model quality needs improvement.
- Target direction: Downward.

### 5. Estimated Effort Reduction

- Definition: Approximate manager hours saved by reducing follow-up overhead.
- Calculation: Estimation based on fewer status collection loops and faster issue escalation.
- Why leadership should track it: Connects the workflow to operational efficiency.
- Target direction: Upward.
